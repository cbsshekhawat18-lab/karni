{% comment %} The base print template of karni-reports. A report's template extends it and fills the blocks: content the body of the report (required) header repeated at the top of every page (default: the report's title) footer repeated at the bottom of every page (default: empty) title the document title (default: the report's title) styles extra CSS, after the base rules page_margin the page margins (default: 22mm 16mm 20mm 16mm) page_label the word before the page number (default: Page) The page size comes from KARNI_REPORTS_PAGE_SIZE ({{ page_size }}).
